# 1.4.0 release notes

One text for App Store Connect. 474 characters, 26 to spare.

```
- Settings has a Backup row: export every counter and tap to one file, and import it back
- Home and a counter's screen roll over to the new day without a relaunch
- A tap after the day turns counts on the new day
- Coming back after iOS closes Cairn in the background no longer shows a frozen screen
- Every theme's text reads clearly, light and dark
- Small buttons answer a tap just outside them
- Toasts sit at the bottom left
- Settings rows stay on one line on a phone
```

The 13 1.4.0 entries from `context/progress/changelog.md` are cut to what an iPhone user sees. Opening on Android and the Android keyboard fix are left out, since this text goes to the App Store only. The minus-after-rollover fix is folded into the tap line, and the best's date, the Export toast wording and the plain-tap toasts are left out to fit; portfolio's changelog carries all of them. Phrases, no period at the end, no examples, as zwipe's are.
