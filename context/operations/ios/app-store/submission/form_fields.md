## Description

Cairn counts the things you do, for life. Name a counter, log how many you did today, and the number goes up and stays up.

A lifetime total on its own is trivia, so every counter also shows this year's total, the average per day, what day of the year it is, and, if you set a goal, whether you're ahead or behind and what today needs to be to catch up. Sixty days of history and a heatmap per counter.

Everything lives on your phone. No account, no server, nothing leaves the device. Export is a CSV of two columns, day and count, because that's all the raw data is.

31 color themes, dark and light, including colorblind-accessible ones.
