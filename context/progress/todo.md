10. Apple Watch, after 1.0.0 is live. The reason the app exists, parked because the hoops are many. Dioxus draws into a WKWebView and watchOS has none, so the watch app is a separate SwiftUI target; none of the UI or orangui carries over. The data model does: `events` is append-only deltas, so a watch that only records taps and ships them home needs no conflict handling. Plan: counters are created and edited on the phone; the phone pushes the counter list over WatchConnectivity (`updateApplicationContext`) and the watch sends taps back as deltas (`transferUserInfo`), which the phone applies to SQLite. No iCloud, so the privacy policy stays true. The work, hardest first: a WCSession bridge on the phone in objc2 (same flavor as `app_icon.rs`, bigger); packaging, since the watch app must be built by Xcode and embedded under `Cairn.app/Watch/` in the hand-assembled IPA, signed inner to outer; two more bundle ids and App Store profiles; then the SwiftUI watch UI itself, which is the small part. First milestone: a dev build where a tap on the watch lands in the phone's database.
